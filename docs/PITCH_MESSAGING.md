# ThreatWeave AI — Presentation Messaging

Use this wording in a pitch deck, project overview, or demo introduction. It describes **decision support**, not automated remediation or verified attack paths.

## Architecture slide

**Title:** ThreatWeave AI Architecture

**Tagline:** Unified Vulnerability and Compliance Prioritization by Correlating Exposure, Compliance, and Business Context

**Short slide subtitle:** From fragmented findings to explainable, context-aware remediation priorities.

## Project overview slide

**Headline:** Turn security findings into defensible decisions.

**Supporting text:** ThreatWeave AI brings together Tenable vulnerabilities, relevant CIS compliance failures, and verified business context. Its deterministic policy engine ranks candidate remediation groups and produces reproducible Decision Receipts that show the supporting evidence and policy contributions.

## 20-second introduction

Security teams face thousands of vulnerability and configuration findings, but scanner scores alone don't capture every business priority. ThreatWeave AI correlates relevant findings on the same asset, adds verified business context, and turns them into ranked remediation candidates. Every decision is explainable, replayable, and subject to analyst review.

## Three points for judges

- **Unify evidence:** Bring vulnerability, compliance, and approved CMDB context into one investigation.
- **Prioritize transparently:** Preserve original Tenable severity/VPR while applying organization-specific decision rules.
- **Demonstrate trust:** Show source references, policy contributions, and reproducible before/after Decision Receipts.

## Suggested demo closing line

**ThreatWeave AI doesn't replace the security analyst's judgment. It makes that judgment faster to review, easier to explain, and possible to reproduce.**

### Claims to avoid

Do not present candidate fix groups as guaranteed one-patch remediations, same-asset correlations as verified attack paths, or synthetic benchmarks as production capacity certifications. ServiceNow live API synchronization and automatic vendor remediation are not implemented.
